# Prototype contract

- Natural bowl-shaped valley, broad route valid at maximum body size, overlapping rock/grove landmarks.
- Continuous player height approximately 0.8?4 metres; nutrient reserve converts to body growth over time.
- Free melee combat; size directly affects strength, health and reach. Defeated creatures drop limited actual resources.
- Three food groves and a Mother Tree. Nutrition can be eaten, transferred or donated to restore the tree.
- NPCs pursue food, safety, control and agreements. World state records grove fertility, stores, controller/access and inhabitants.
- AI conversation must execute validated gameplay actions; no invented completed transfers, kills or promises without an executor.
- Restoration reveals the current society/ecology rather than selecting a canned ending. Restart must work.

## First playable scope

One valley, three principal NPC personalities, a few simpler creatures, one food type, light/heavy melee, dodge, collection, consumption, donation, actual AI negotiation, observable NPC harvest/flight/migration.

## Milestones

1. Independent project + documented art/API budget.
2. Terrain, character, camera, continuous growth and first standalone build.
3. Combat, food economy, Mother Tree and outcome metrics.
4. NPC resource behaviour and executable AI agreements.
5. Consistent art, audio/UI, packaged playtest and submission files.

Each milestone is committed and pushed when reviewable, before the next major milestone. Never rewrite commit dates or import old history.
